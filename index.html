The problem

Loan files get delayed when workflow and decisioning live apart.

Manual handoffs

RM, credit, verification, sanction, and OPS teams lose time tracking ownership across calls, messages, and spreadsheets.

Policy leakage

Credit policies, deviation rules, FOIR/LTV checks, and approval matrices are hard to enforce when rules sit outside the workflow.

Slow underwriting

Applications wait for KYC, bureau, bank statement review, document checks, and manual credit notes.

Missing audit trail

Operations teams need clear proof of who moved a case, what changed, and why a decision was made.

Disconnected decisioning

AI scores, business rules, fraud signals, and reviewer comments often live in different systems.

Disbursement risk

Files reach OPS without complete checklists, mandates, penny-drop status, or final maker-checker evidence.
